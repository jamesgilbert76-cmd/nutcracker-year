    Questions

    Good to know

    Will it really arrive before Christmas?

    Yes. Orders placed by our cut-off date are guaranteed to arrive before 20 December (US addresses). We’ll show the exact cut-off before you order.

    Her performance is after the cut-off. What about show photos?

    Your Christmas copy tells the story through casting, rehearsals, dress rehearsal and costume photos. If you’d like performance photos too, choose the “Curtain Call” option and we’ll print after the final bow and ship in January.

    Does my child have to be a Mouse?

    No. Choose Mouse, Angel, Soldier, Snowflake, Clara, Party Child, Flower, Sugar Plum Fairy and more. The words and pictures change to match her role.

    Will the illustrations look like my child?

    The illustrated dancer is a gentle storybook character, not a portrait. You choose her hair colour, skin tone and costume colours, and your own photos go on the photo pages.

    Is this made with our ballet school?

    No. It’s an independent keepsake, and it doesn’t use any studio or production logos. Teachers and schools who’d like to offer it to families can get in touch.

    What happens to my photos?

    They’re used only to make your book, and deleted after it ships unless you ask us to keep them for a reprint.
